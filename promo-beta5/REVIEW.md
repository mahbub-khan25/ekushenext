# Review — v1 (website film, 47.7 s)

- Built storyline C (one continuous take through the blue suggestion chips) with A's story (one son,
  one chat with Ma, one day; Ma answers from Dhaka). B was not built.
- Every typed line and every suggestion is the engine's real output (assets/trace.js from the
  demo_trace example); the bars are the beta.5 apps' own designs; the Android keys are a crop of the
  app's own screenshot.
- Checked: `hyperframes check` (no errors besides the vertical root, which now lives in variants/),
  snapshots of every scene and every seam in both sizes, the rendered files' loudness (−16 LUFS,
  true peak −1.2 dB, no clipping). The music was measured, not heard.
- Weakest shot: the Windows/Mac establishing frames, where the device is wide and the chat window
  mostly empty; with more time the chat would carry a day of earlier messages.
- Kept on purpose: the suggestion chips change faster than a word can be read while a word is
  typed (that is what the bar does).
