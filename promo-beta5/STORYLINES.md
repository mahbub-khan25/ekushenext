# Ekushe Next v0.2.0-beta.5 promo — three storylines

For the release and the website's film (replacing the beta.24-era film, whose suggestion strip and
keyboard no longer look like the app). 16:9 1920×1080 30 fps for the website and YouTube, plus a 9:16
cut for Reels/Shorts and the website's phone view. Music only, no voice; music made locally as for
the earlier films (nothing paid).

## A. মায়ের কাছে একটি বার্তা (recommended: a person and a moment everyone knows)

Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: object-to-object chain.
A son in Toronto, late at night, types "ma, kemon acho?" on his Windows laptop; the new frosted bar
offers কেমন আছো, Space places it. The same sentence travels: the Mac at work, the Android phone on
the bus, his tablet with its keyboard cover in desktop mode. In Dhaka his mother reads it on her phone
and answers in Bangla. Each device is entered through the last one's object (the chip becomes the
next screen).

- First 2 s: a laptop at night, "11:58 PM · Toronto", the cursor in a chat box, `ma, kemon acho` being
  typed and the bar already offering মা, কেমন আছো.
- Beats: hook (the message) → tension (the old way: gibberish Bijoy text, a Shift chart) → reveal
  (একুশে নেক্সট) → proof (Windows bar, Mac bar, Android phone, tablet + physical keyboard, Backspace
  reopens a word, `as+te` → আস্তে) → payoff (mother's reply arrives) → claim (যেভাবে বলেন, সেভাবে
  লিখুন) → end card (logo, free on Windows · Mac · Linux · Android, ekushenext.com).
- Look: the product's own light frosted glass and Ekushe Default blue over real-world grounds (a
  night desk, a sunlit Dhaka window); Hind Siliguri for titles.
- Sound: a warm piano and tabla bed, soft key clicks on every typed letter, a rise on the reply.

## B. ঠিক বানান, যেভাবেই লিখুন

Device: life before and after, word by word. Form: cut on the beat.
Giant Roman words people really type (`byabostha`, `khaiso`, `rahim`, `as+te`, `office`) fill the
frame and snap into the standard spelling on the beat, each inside the real suggestion bar of a
different device. Ends on the dictionary's real numbers.

- First 2 s: `byabostha` in huge letters, the chip row flips, ব্যবস্থা lands blue.
- Beats: hook (one word fixed) → tension (wrong spellings online) → reveal → proof (6 words, 6 devices)
  → payoff (a whole sentence typed in chat style comes out প্রমিত) → claim → end card.
- Look: flag green and white fields cut on the beat, the bars as the only UI.
- Sound: a driving dhol loop, a hit on each snap.

## C. একটি বার, সব পর্দা (the bold one)

Device: the feature is the hook. Form: one continuous take, an endless zoom.
The camera never cuts: it starts inside one chip of the suggestion bar, pulls back to Windows, dives
into the blue chip, which opens into the Mac bar, then the Android strip, then the tablet strip
lifting above its taskbar, then out to the whole world of devices.

- First 2 s: a single blue chip আমার fills the frame, then the camera pulls back.
- Beats: hook → reveal → proof (each zoom is a device and one new feature) → payoff (all devices at
  once) → claim → end card.
- Look: one light glass material at every scale.
- Sound: a long rising pad, a whoosh on each zoom.
